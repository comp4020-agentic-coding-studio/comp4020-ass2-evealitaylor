## The newcomer cliff

Roughly **9 in 10** first-time contributors never come back after their first
month.

The 1 in 10 who stay do not look like the population they document.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Deciding what gets removed is deciding what the place is.

## What "unpaid" is doing here

Not one thing. At least four:

1. a gift
2. a hobby
3. an obligation
4. a second job you did not apply for

Week 9 is about the fourth.

---

## Twelve weeks

1. **Newcomer** — read the room, make one true edit
2. **Contributor** — get reverted, argue it, close a deletion
3. **Steward** — write rules, staff the queue, design the rota
4. **Handover** — strike, fork, audit, leave properly

---

## Before week 2

- an account you are willing to keep
- a project you will stay with until week 12
- the contributor undertaking, signed
