Nobody below was trained to do the work they teach, because there is no
training for it. They learned it the way week 3 will teach you: badly, in
public, corrected by strangers who owed them nothing.

Read them before week 1, when you choose a home project. Each teaches a
different theory of what counts as evidence. Prue's project asks whether a
claim is published. Ivy's asks whether you have been there. Ren's asks whether
the person you removed can find out why.
