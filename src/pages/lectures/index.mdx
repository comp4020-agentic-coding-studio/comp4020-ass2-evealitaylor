One lecture a week, each one setting up the Shift it shares a date with. They
run to the same arc the course does: three weeks learning to be a newcomer,
three learning to be a contributor, three learning to hold the mop, and three on
what happens when you put it down.

Week 1 carries [slides](/decks/week-01/). The rest carry the argument, and the
work is in the Shift.
