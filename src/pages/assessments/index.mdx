# Assessment

Four pieces of work. Two of them ask you to write about communities you did
not join, and two of them mark what you actually did in the ones you did.

The Contribution Log runs from week 2 and is the spine — everything a Shift
produces ends up in it. The other three are due once. Nothing here is an exam,
because there is no body of content to examine you on; the material is what
happened when you contributed.
