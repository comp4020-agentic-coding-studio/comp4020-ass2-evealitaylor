Something here was renamed, removed, or never written. This site keeps no
record of which, and that is the failure week 8 spends two hours on: a removal
with no reason attached is indistinguishable from a thing that was never there.

There is no appeal, because nothing on this page was decided by a person.

What you can do is the check week 5 asks for on every citation in a sourcing
dossier. Follow the link back to wherever you found it, and see whether it has
rotted or whether the content simply moved.
