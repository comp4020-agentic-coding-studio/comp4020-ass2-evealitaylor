The week the course stops being about reading.

## Before the Shift

Identify three candidate contributions from your norm map — things that are
wrong, missing or broken, and that you can fix with evidence your project's
standard accepts. Small. A date, a dead link, a misclassified road, a typo in
a docstring.

## In the Shift

Make the edit. Write the summary line before you save, not after, because the
summary is what a reviewer reads first and most of them read nothing else.

Then write a prediction in your log: will this survive, and if it is reverted,
by whom and on what grounds? Sealed predictions are marked in week 4 against
what actually happened, and being wrong is worth more than being right if you
can say why.

Ivy will take anyone on OSM through their first changeset; be warned that the
bar for evidence there is higher than you think and "the council website says
so" is not going to clear it.

## Afterwards

Watch it. Add the page to your watchlist, subscribe to the changeset, follow
the pull request. Whatever happens next is week 4's material.
