The last Shift, and the only deliverable in this course written for people you
will never meet.

## Before the Shift

Your Governance Redesign is due at noon. Bring the rationale register, the week
10 dissent, and the week 11 audit.

## In the Shift

Write the handover. What exists, what it does, what was decided, what was
decided against and why, which rules exist because of one specific incident in
week 8, and who to ask when the register does not cover it. Assume the reader is
competent, has no context, and will not be able to reach you.

Then the part most volunteers skip. Go back to your home project and post a
closing note: what you did, what you left unfinished, and whether you are
staying. Some of you will be staying, and the honest version of that sentence is
worth more than a polite one.

We end by reading the 2026 cohort's handover note aloud, including the parts
that turned out to be wrong, and then sealing yours for the 2028 cohort to do
the same to.

## Afterwards

The sandbox passes on. The Continuity of Service policy requires it, and for
once the policy is right.
