Not a debate. A deletion discussion, which is a different thing, and the
difference is that nobody wins it.

## Before the Shift

Your Conflict Autopsy is due at noon today. Bring it submitted, not in progress.

Read the notability guideline for your project and the three most recent
discussions closed under it.

## In the Shift

We run a real one. Prue nominates a genuinely borderline subject — a person, a
place, a piece of software with three passing mentions and no depth — and the
cohort argues it to the project's own procedure, over the clock, in writing.

One of you closes. You do not count votes. You weigh arguments against policy,
discount the ones that are assertions of importance, and write a rationale that
the losing side can read without concluding you were biased.

Then we look at the same nomination from the other end: who tends to get
nominated, what the keep-rates look like across subject areas, and whether a
correctly applied rule produced a defensible result today.

## Afterwards

The closing rationale is logged. Whoever closed is excused the following week's
log entry, which is the only mercy this course offers.
