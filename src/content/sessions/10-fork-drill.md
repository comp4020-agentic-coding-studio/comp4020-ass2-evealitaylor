A decision, not a discussion. There is a clock, and at the end of it the cohort
has to have done something.

## Before the Shift

Read Ren's account of June 2023, posted to the sandbox. It is the long version
and it is not flattering to anyone in it.

## In the Shift

The scenario lands at the start of the Shift and you have ninety minutes. The
sandbox's host has announced a change that the cohort broadly objects to. Your
options are the real ones: comply, protest, go dark, or fork and take the
community with you.

Work the costs. Going dark means the queue is unattended and the people you
protect are exposed for the duration — that cost falls on them, not on the party
you are bargaining with, and every honest account of 2023 has to start there.
Forking means the data, the tooling and the people, and you cost all three
before you claim it is an option.

Whatever the cohort decides, the dissent is written down by the dissenters, in
their words, and kept.

## Afterwards

Recorded in the Log, including by the people who lost the vote. In week 12 you
will hand this record to next year's cohort.
