Fieldwork. You will spend most of this Shift reading things other people got
told off for.

## Before the Shift

Pull thirty days of your project's public record. On Wikipedia that is the
article histories and talk archives for a topic area you care about; on Reddit
the modlog and removed submissions; on OSM the changeset comments and
reverts; on a repository the closed-without-merge pull requests.

## In the Shift

Build the map in two columns. On the left, what the community says its rules
are. On the right, what it actually removes. Work only from decisions — a rule
is enforced if you can point at a case, and a rule you believe in without a case
is a hunch and goes in a third column marked as such.

The delta is the finding. Expect it to be large, and expect at least one rule in
it to be uncomfortable to state plainly.

Pair up for the last thirty minutes and read someone else's map for a different
project. The rules will differ; the delta will not.

## Afterwards

The norm map is the first entry in your Contribution Log, and week 3's edit is
supposed to survive contact with it.
