Bench work. One claim, two epistemologies, and the afternoon it takes to find
out they cannot be reconciled.

## Before the Shift

Choose a claim about a physical thing in the world: a road, a building, a
boundary, a closure. Something you could in principle go and look at, and that
somebody has also published about.

## In the Shift

Build the dossier twice. Once to Wikipedia's standard — published, independent,
attributable, and you are forbidden from relying on having been there. Once to
OSM's — survey, trace, imagery, local knowledge, and a newspaper counts for
almost nothing.

Somewhere in the middle you will find a source that one project trusts
completely and the other will not touch. Write it down. That source is the
course's whole argument about provenance, compressed into a footnote.

Last half hour: link rot. Run your citations, find the dead ones, archive the
live ones. About one in nine of Wikipedia's is already gone, and more than half
its articles have at least one.

## Afterwards

The dossier goes in the Log. In week 6 you will watch someone's article get
deleted over exactly this kind of disagreement.
