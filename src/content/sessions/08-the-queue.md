Your first shift on a real queue, with real consequences for people in the room
with you.

## Before the Shift

Re-read the ruleset you wrote. You will be enforcing it against classmates who
helped write it and will remember which parts you pushed for.

## In the Shift

You are rostered in pairs, forty minutes on the queue and forty on appeals, and
you do not hear appeals against your own decisions. ShiftBot has already removed
what it removed; your first job is deciding which of those it got wrong.

Write every removal reason in full. Rule, quoted evidence, consequence, whether
they can come back. Ren marks these the way moderator applications get marked,
which is unsentimentally.

Then you sit on the other side and read appeals against your classmates'
decisions, and you will discover that a removal which felt obvious to write is
often impossible to defend to a third party.

## Afterwards

The queue does not stop when the Shift does. Enforcement continues, rostered,
until week 12 — which is the point of week 9.
