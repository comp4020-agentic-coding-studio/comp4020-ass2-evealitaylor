Six years on the mod team of a default-adjacent subreddit with 2.1 million
subscribers, four of them as the person who wrote the AutoModerator config.
At peak we were removing about 900 comments a day between nine volunteers,
none of whom had met, on a rota that existed because I made a spreadsheet.

I left in June 2023 during the API protest, along with most of the team. What
I actually think about that month is complicated and I will tell you the
complicated version in week 10, not the version that got upvoted.

The thing I am here to teach is narrower than politics: a removal is a message
to a person. Most moderators never learn to write one, because the interface
does not ask them to. In the enforcement weeks you will write several hundred,
and I will mark them the way I marked applicants to my own team.
