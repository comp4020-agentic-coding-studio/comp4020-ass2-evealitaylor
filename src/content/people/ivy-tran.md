I map roads. Specifically, I have spent eleven years adding and correcting
roads in northern Vietnam and the Mekong Delta on OpenStreetMap, and five of
those coordinating remote mapping teams after floods, where a track that exists
on the map and not on the ground can send an ambulance somewhere it cannot
turn around.

This is where the course gets its standard of evidence. Wikipedia asks whether
a claim is verifiable in a published source. OSM asks whether you have been
there, or have imagery that shows it, and it does not accept a citation as a
substitute for either. Two projects, two theories of truth, and students who
can hold both at once are the ones who can move between communities without
causing damage.

I also handle the part of week 11 about imported data and machine edits, which
is the fight OSM has been having, politely, since 2009.
