Registered an account in 2006 to fix a date in an article about a closed
railway line, and did not stop. Sixty-three thousand edits later: mostly
gazetteer work on Australian rail infrastructure, a long stretch at Articles
for Deletion, three years clerking at Requests for Adminship, and one topic ban
from the Arbitration Committee in 2014 which I served, appealed, and lost.

I teach the appeal in week 8. Not as a cautionary tale — the sanction was
correctly applied and I would apply it myself now — but because being on the
receiving end of a process you believe in is the hardest thing this course asks
of you, and it seemed unfair to ask it without having done it.

My research is on the demographics of who stays. Volunteer projects lose
roughly nine of every ten newcomers inside a month, and the ten percent who
remain look nothing like the population they document. That is the problem the
course is really about.
